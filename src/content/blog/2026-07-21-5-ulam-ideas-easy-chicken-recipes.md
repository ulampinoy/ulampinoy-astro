## The Five Ulam Ideas

### 1. Lechon Manok (Roast Chicken)

Golden, herb-rubbed, and irresistibly juicy—**lechon manok** made at home is easier than you think. Slow-cook it low and gentle to keep every drop of juice inside, then finish under the grill for that crispy, browned skin. It is a whole-chicken centerpiece that turns any meal into a feast. See our full guide to [homemade lechon manok](/blog/chicken-roast-crockpot).

**Ingredients**

- 1 whole chicken (about 1.2–1.5 kg)
- 1 tablespoon thyme
- 1 tablespoon rosemary
- 1 tablespoon oregano
- 1/2 tablespoon paprika
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed black pepper
- 1 whole lemon, cut into wedges

### 2. Chicken and Bell Peppers

A quick, colorful stir-fry of tender chicken tossed with sweet **bell peppers** and onions. It comes together in minutes and brings both crunch and color to the table—perfect for those nights when you want something bright, fresh, and fast over hot rice.

**Ingredients**

- 1/2 kg chicken (fillet or thighs), cut into bite-size cubes
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 green bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 medium onion, sliced into wedges
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch (dissolved in 2 tablespoons water)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil

### 3. Creamy Mushroom Chicken

For a richer treat, chicken thighs simmered in a **creamy mushroom sauce** deliver comfort in every bite. The earthy mushrooms and velvety sauce cling to each piece, making it feel special without any fuss. Craving more? Try our [chicken with mushrooms in blue cheese sauce](/blog/chicken-mushrooms-blue-cheese-sauce).

**Ingredients**

- 1/2 kg chicken thigh fillets (deboned)
- 1 cup mushrooms (button or assorted), sliced
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon mixed herbs (oregano, thyme, rosemary), optional
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil for frying

### 4. Chicken Afritada

A classic one-pot Filipino stew, **chicken afritada** braises chicken in a savory tomato sauce with potatoes, carrots, and bell peppers. It is hearty, homey, and even better the next day—great for _baon_ and family dinners alike. Here is our [chicken afritada recipe](/blog/chicken-afritada).

**Ingredients**

- 1 kg chicken, cut into serving pieces
- 2 medium potatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 large carrot, cut into chunks
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 green bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce (patis)
- 1/2 cup green peas
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil (olive or canola)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### 5. Arroz Caldo (Chicken Rice Porridge)

When you need something warm and gentle, nothing beats **arroz caldo**—a thick chicken-and-rice porridge topped with a soft egg, toasted garlic, spring onions, and a squeeze of calamansi. It is the ultimate rainy-day comfort food and a favorite merienda or light dinner.

**Ingredients**

- 1/2 kg chicken, cut into serving pieces
- 1 cup glutinous rice (malagkit) or regular rice
- 6–8 cups water or chicken broth
- 1 thumb-size ginger, julienned
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, minced (plus extra for toasted garlic topping)
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce (patis)
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

**To serve**

- Hard-boiled or soft-boiled eggs, halved
- Toasted garlic
- Spring onions, chopped
- Calamansi wedges
